# UnityHub visual identity

## Logo

The UnityHub mark combines an interlocking **U/H monogram**, a central cross, and a rising sweep. The monogram represents a connected worship team; the cross identifies the Christian worship context; the sweep suggests forward motion and shared purpose.

Primary artwork: `app/src/main/res/drawable/unityhub_mark.xml` (Android vector) and `docs/brand/unityhub-mark.svg` (scalable SVG).

## Color system

UnityHub uses a calm, natural palette built around a warm paper-like canvas and grounded botanical accents.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Butter cream | `#F5EBD8` | Main light-theme canvas |
| Warm cream | `#FFF9EF` | Cards, fields, and light surfaces |
| Forest green | `#315C46` | Primary actions, navigation, and brand identity |
| Deep forest | `#193B2B` | Dark-theme canvas, icon background, and high-emphasis surfaces |
| Terracotta | `#A7523B` | Secondary emphasis, highlights, and tertiary actions |
| Muted gold | `#B59A5A` | Supporting accents, status details, and secondary actions |

The signature brand blend moves **forest green → terracotta → muted gold**. Use each color with restraint: forest green anchors the interface, butter cream provides warmth and breathing room, terracotta creates human warmth, and muted gold adds a quiet highlight. Avoid returning to the old crimson/coral/amber treatment or introducing purple and indigo into the brand system.

Light and dark appearances use the same four brand colors with adjusted contrast-safe tints. Do not use muted gold as small text on a cream surface; use deep forest text on gold controls and surfaces.

## Clear space and use

- Keep at least 12% of the mark width clear around the icon when placing it beside text.
- Use the full-color mark on light or dark neutral surfaces; the mark itself carries its near-black rounded-square tile.
- Use the adaptive icon foreground and dark background as separate layers for Android launchers.
- Use the single-color white version for Android monochrome themed icons and status-bar notification glyphs.
- Do not stretch, recolor individual letters, or place the mark over a busy photograph.

## Wordmark

Use **Unity** in the current surface text color and **Hub** in the forest-green-to-terracotta-to-muted-gold gradient. Recommended descriptor: “PLAN · LEAD · WORSHIP · TOGETHER”.
